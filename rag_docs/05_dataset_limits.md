# The training dataset and its limits
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.010

The model was trained on the chest X-ray dataset published by Kermany and colleagues in 2018. It contains about 5,860 front-view chest X-rays of children aged one to five, all from a single hospital: Guangzhou Women and Children's Medical Center in China. Expert doctors graded the images as normal or pneumonia.

Because all images come from children at one hospital, the model has not been tested on adults, on other hospitals, or on different X-ray machines. Its performance on those images is unknown and is likely to be worse. The dataset also has about three times more pneumonia images than normal ones.
