# Why a chest X-ray alone cannot confirm pneumonia
Source: https://radiopaedia.org/articles/pneumonia

A white area on a chest X-ray is not specific to pneumonia. Collapsed lung (atelectasis), fluid around the lung (pleural effusion), fluid in the lungs from heart problems, and some tumours can look similar. Very early pneumonia can also look almost normal on an X-ray.

For this reason, doctors combine the X-ray with the patient's symptoms (such as fever, cough and fast or difficult breathing), a physical examination and sometimes blood tests. An X-ray finding is one piece of evidence, not a diagnosis on its own.
