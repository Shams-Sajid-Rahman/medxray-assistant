# Pneumonia in children
Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection of the lungs. According to the World Health Organization, it is the single largest infectious cause of death in children worldwide. It can be caused by bacteria, such as Streptococcus pneumoniae, or by viruses, such as respiratory syncytial virus (RSV).

Common signs in children include cough, fever, and fast or difficult breathing. Many cases can be prevented with vaccination, good nutrition and reduced indoor air pollution, and bacterial pneumonia can be treated with antibiotics prescribed by a health worker.
